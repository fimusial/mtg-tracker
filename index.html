Log Files

  • Before using the tracker, make sure you have started MTG Arena and enabled Detailed Logs (Options > Account)
  • Tracker reads all information as a live stream from Player.log and Player-prev.log.
  • Collection/Inventory information updates are visible only after MTG Arena is restarted.
  • The log files must be located at: %USERPROFILE%/AppData/LocalLow/Wizards Of The Coast/MTGA
